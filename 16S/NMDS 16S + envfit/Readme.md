To visualize the relationships among soil bacterial community structure, management intensity, environmental variables, and bacterial functional guilds, a three-dimensional non-metric multidimensional scaling (NMDS) ordination was performed using the Aitchison distance matrix of 16S soil bacterial communities. Management intensity was coded as an ordinal gradient from forest to highly managed urban sites.

Environmental variables and CLR-transformed bacterial functional guild abundances were fitted onto the NMDS ordination using envfit with 999 permutations. Only significant vectors were retained for visualization. This approach was used to identify soil physicochemical variables and bacterial functional guilds associated with major axes of variation in soil bacterial community composition. NMDS stress was used to evaluate ordination quality.
