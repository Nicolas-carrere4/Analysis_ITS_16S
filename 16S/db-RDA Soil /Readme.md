To identify environmental drivers of soil bacterial community composition, analyses were conducted on soil samples only. Aitchison distances calculated from CLR-transformed 16S ASV abundances were used as the community dissimilarity matrix. Soil physicochemical variables, including temperature, moisture, pH, soil organic matter (SOM), ammonium (NH4), nitrate (NO3), and Olsen phosphorus, were matched to each soil sample using Tree_ID.

Environmental variables were standardized prior to analysis. Marginal effects of each soil variable on bacterial community composition were tested using distance-based PERMANOVA with 999 permutations. To visualize the multivariate relationships between soil bacterial communities and environmental gradients, distance-based redundancy analysis (db-RDA) was performed using the same Aitchison distance matrix and standardized environmental variables. Variance inflation factors were calculated to assess collinearity among environmental predictors.
