Environmental drivers of bacterial soil community composition were assessed using Aitchison distances calculated from CLR-normalized 16S community data. Analyses were restricted to soil samples. Environmental variables, including temperature, moisture, pH, soil organic matter, ammonium, nitrate, and Olsen phosphorus, were standardized prior to analysis.

The marginal contribution of each environmental variable to bacterial community composition was first evaluated using PERMANOVA with 999 permutations. Distance-based redundancy analysis (db-RDA) was then performed to identify the main environmental gradients associated with community variation. Overall model significance and the marginal effects of individual environmental predictors were assessed using permutation tests. Variance inflation factors were additionally examined to evaluate collinearity among explanatory variables.
